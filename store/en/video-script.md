# Logseq Copilot 2.0: intro video script

Silent, 54 seconds, 7 scenes. Record in Chrome, light mode, with Logseq running and a graph that has notes on the topic searched in scene 1. Each caption is at most 8 words.

## Scene 1 (0:00-0:08) Search results

On screen: a DuckDuckGo results page for a query that matches notes in the graph, such as "zettelkasten". The Logseq Copilot card sits in the right column. It shows the graph name, matching pages with their best blocks, and a gray parent line above a nested block.

Caption: Your Logseq notes, next to your search

## Scene 2 (0:08-0:14) Open a note

On screen: the pointer clicks a block on the card. The Logseq desktop app comes to the front at that block.

Caption: Click a note to open it in Logseq

## Scene 3 (0:14-0:23) Sidebar for the page

On screen: an article in a browser tab. The pointer clicks the Logseq Copilot toolbar icon. The Chrome side panel opens with the sidebar: the Search Logseq box, the Clip page and Clip selection buttons, and below them the notes in the graph that mention the page.

Caption: Notes about the page you are reading

## Scene 4 (0:23-0:32) Clip a selection

On screen: the pointer selects a paragraph of the article, then clicks Clip selection in the sidebar header. The paragraph turns teal on the page. The new highlight appears at the top of the sidebar list as a quote.

Caption: Clip a selection into your journal

## Scene 5 (0:32-0:39) The clip in Logseq

On screen: today's journal page in Logseq. The last block is the clip from the default template: the Clip tag, the linked page title, and the clipped paragraph below.

Caption: Shaped by your own template

## Scene 6 (0:39-0:47) Comment on a highlight

On screen: back in the article, the pointer clicks the teal highlight. The comment popup opens next to it. The pointer clicks Edit, types a short comment, and clicks Save.

Caption: Add a comment to any highlight

## Scene 7 (0:47-0:54) End card

On screen: the Logseq Copilot icon, the name "Logseq Copilot", the tagline from tagline.txt ("Your Logseq notes, right where you browse"), and the URL logseq-copilot.eindex.me. Below it: Chrome, Edge, Firefox.

Caption: none. The end card text is the content.
