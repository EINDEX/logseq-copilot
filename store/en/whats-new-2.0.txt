Logseq Copilot 2.0 is a rebuild of the extension.

- The toolbar icon opens a sidebar for the page: its highlights, the notes that mention it, a search of your graph and clip buttons. It replaces the popup.
- The card on search result pages is redesigned. Brave Search is a new built-in engine.
- Recall rules can also recall notes about the parent paths of a page.
- Clips use your graph's journal date format. A failed clip tells you why.
- Firefox 127 or later is required.

Your settings carry over.
