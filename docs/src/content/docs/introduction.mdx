# Logseq Copilot

**Logseq Copilot** is a browser extension that bridges the gap between your web browsing experience and your Logseq personal knowledge base. It allows you to seamlessly capture, organize, and recall information while browsing the web.

## Key Features

### 🔍 Smart Note Recall

Search your Logseq notes when you search in your browser. Your personal knowledge base becomes accessible from any webpage.

### 📝 Quick Capture with Highlights

Record highlights with your ideas immediately. Add personal notes and comments to any text on the web.

### 📔 Contextual Notes

Search your Logseq notes based on the tabs you are currently using. Find relevant information contextually.

### 🎯 Highlights Sidebar

See the highlights of the page you are reading in your browser's own sidebar. Jump to a highlight, edit its note, or delete it.

## Why Logseq Copilot?

- **Seamless Integration**: Works with your existing Logseq workflow
- **Privacy Focused**: Your data stays in your Logseq instance
- **Cross-Browser Support**: Available on all major browsers
- **Real-time Sync**: Changes sync instantly with your Logseq database
