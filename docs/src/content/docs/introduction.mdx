**Logseq Copilot** is a browser extension that connects your browser to your own [Logseq](https://logseq.com). It brings the notes you already wrote to the pages you read and search, and it sends what you clip back into your graph.

It talks to the HTTP API server of the Logseq desktop app on your computer. Nothing goes through a service of ours.

## How It Works

Logseq Copilot shows up in four places.

### Search engine pages

When you search on Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo, Kagi and other supported engines, a panel next to the results lists the blocks and pages of your graph that match your keywords. See [Search Engines](/features/search-engines/).

### The sidebar and the toolbar icon

Click the toolbar icon on any web page and the sidebar opens beside it, with the notes of your graph that mention the address of that page. The icon also carries a badge with the number of matching notes. You can search by keyword in the sidebar too; clear the search box to see the notes for the page again. The graph name at the top opens the graph in Logseq. What counts as a match is tunable per website with [Recall Rules](/features/recall-rules/). See [Sidebar](/features/highlights-sidebar/).

### Clipping

Clip the text you selected, or just the link of the page, into your journal, the current page or any page, shaped by a template. See [Clipping & Templates](/features/clipping-and-templates/).

### Highlights

A clipped selection is also saved as a highlight. It is painted on the page the next time you open it, you can click it to add a comment, and the highlights of the page you are reading are listed in the sidebar. See [Sidebar](/features/highlights-sidebar/) and [Highlight Comments](/features/highlight-comments/).

## Why Logseq Copilot?

- **File graphs and DB graphs**: works with Logseq 0.10 (file graph) and Logseq 2.x (DB graph). It detects which one is open.
- **Private**: it only talks to the Logseq API server you set up, which is on your own computer by default. Highlights are stored in your browser.
- **Chrome, Edge and Firefox**: Firefox 127 or later. Safari is not supported.
- **Light and dark**: the interface follows the theme of the page and of your system.
