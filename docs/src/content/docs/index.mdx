## Key Features

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="🔍 Smart Note Recall" icon="magnifier">
    Search your Logseq notes when you search in your browser. Your personal knowledge base becomes accessible from any webpage.
  </Card>
  <Card title="📝 Quick Capture" icon="pencil">
    Record highlights with your ideas immediately. Add personal notes and comments to any text on the web.
  </Card>
  <Card title="📔 Contextual Notes" icon="open-book">
    Search your Logseq notes based on the tabs you are currently using. Find relevant information contextually.
  </Card>
  <Card title="🎯 Highlights Sidebar" icon="bars">
    View and manage all your highlights in a convenient sidebar. Filter by current page or browse all your saved highlights.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
