## Why Sponsor?

Your sponsorship helps:

- **🚀 Development**: Fund new features and improvements
- **🐛 Bug Fixes**: Ensure quick resolution of issues
- **📚 Documentation**: Maintain comprehensive guides and tutorials
- **🔧 Maintenance**: Keep the extension compatible with browser updates
- **💡 Innovation**: Research and implement cutting-edge features

## Sponsor Benefits

### For Monthly Sponsors

- **Priority Support**: Get faster responses to your issues
- **Early Access**: Try new features before public release
- **Direct Feedback**: Influence the development roadmap
- **Recognition**: Be listed as a sponsor (optional)

### For All Sponsors

- **Warm Fuzzy Feeling**: Know you're supporting open-source development
- **Better Extension**: Help make Logseq Copilot even better
- **Community**: Join a community of supporters

### Community Engagement

- 💬 **Join discussions** on [GitHub](https://github.com/EINDEX/logseq-copilot/discussions)

## Transparency

We believe in transparency about how sponsorship funds are used:

- **Development Time**: Compensating for time spent coding and maintaining
- **Infrastructure**: Hosting costs, domain names, and development tools
- **Browser Store Fees**: Registration and maintenance fees for extension stores
- **Testing Devices**: Ensuring compatibility across different platforms
