# Highlights Sidebar

The highlights sidebar lists the highlights you saved on the page you are reading. From there you can jump to a highlight, edit its note, or delete it.

It opens in your browser's own sidebar: the **side panel** in Chrome and Edge, and the **sidebar** in Firefox. Nothing is drawn on top of the web page.

## Opening the Sidebar

There are three ways to open it:

1. **Popup button**: Click the Logseq Copilot icon in the toolbar, then click **Highlights**.
2. **Right-click menu**: Right-click on the page, or on selected text, and choose **Open highlights sidebar**.
3. **Keyboard shortcut**: Press your **Open highlights sidebar** shortcut. It has no default key, so you need to set one first.

### Setting the Keyboard Shortcut

The shortcut is unbound by default, because any key combination can clash with the browser's own shortcuts or with another extension. Choose your own in the browser's shortcut settings:

- **Chrome**: Open `chrome://extensions/shortcuts`, find Logseq Copilot, and set a key for **Open highlights sidebar**.
- **Edge**: Open `edge://extensions/shortcuts` and do the same.
- **Firefox**: Open `about:addons`, click the gear icon, choose **Manage Extension Shortcuts**, and set a key for **Open highlights sidebar** under Logseq Copilot.

:::note
Browsers do not allow links to their settings pages. Copy the address into the address bar of a new tab.
:::

## What the Sidebar Shows

- **The page in the active tab**: The highlights saved for the exact address of that page, newest first. The header shows the page title and how many highlights it has.
- **It follows the active tab**: Switch to another tab, or open another page in the same tab, and the list changes to match. The sidebar stays open, so you do not need to close and reopen it.
- **Each highlight** shows the highlighted text, your note (if any), the site, when you created it, and its tags.
- **A page without highlights** shows "No highlights on this page".

To close the sidebar, use the close button the browser gives its side panel or sidebar.

## Working with Highlights

Move the pointer over a highlight, or move to it with the keyboard, to show its buttons.

- **Locate it on the page**: Click the highlight. The page scrolls to the highlighted text and flashes it briefly.
- **Edit the note**: Click the pencil button, type the note, then click **Save** (or press `Ctrl+Enter` / `Cmd+Enter`). Click **Cancel** or press `Escape` to drop your changes. If the comment popup of the same highlight is open on the page, it shows the new note.
- **Delete it**: Click the trash button. The highlight is removed from the list and from the page right away. There is no confirmation and no undo.

Highlights are created on the page itself by clipping a selection. See [Highlight Comments](/features/highlight-comments) for how to create them.

## Pages Without Highlights

Highlights work on web pages only, the ones whose address starts with `http://` or `https://`. On any other page, such as the new tab page, browser pages like `chrome://extensions` or `about:addons`, local files, or the pages of other extensions, the sidebar shows **Highlights aren't available on this page**. Open a web page and the list appears on its own.

## Troubleshooting

**"Reload the page to jump to highlights"**:

You clicked a highlight, but the page is not running the extension, so the extension could not scroll to the text. This usually means the page was already open when you installed or updated Logseq Copilot: the extension only attaches to a page when it loads. Reload the page and click the highlight again. On a page where the browser does not let extensions run at all, reloading does not help.

**"Couldn't find this highlight on the page"**:

The extension could not find the highlighted text on the page, usually because the content of the page changed since you saved it. The highlight stays in the list, and you can still edit or delete it.
